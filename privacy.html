Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Ila Fintech ("we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact and quote forms: your name, email address, phone number, company, the service you are interested in, your budget range and your message.
  • Job applications: your name, contact details, the role you are interested in, a link to your CV or portfolio and anything you tell us about yourself.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and to keep the site running.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare quotes.
  • To deliver and support the services you ask for.
  • To consider your job application.
  • To keep this website secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send you marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Services that help run this website

Messages you send through our forms are delivered to our inbox by a form delivery service (FormSubmit), which processes the message only to deliver it. The website uses Google Fonts, which means your browser loads font files from Google's servers. Links to Google Maps and social media open those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies for tracking or advertising. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and project emails for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and accounting requirements. Job applications are kept for up to one year unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

In line with the Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018) of Nepal, you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To do this, email info@ilafintech.com.

Security

This website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us, but no method of sending data over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
