How local businesses in Nepal can rank on Google

Simple SEO steps any shop, clinic or school can start this week.

Digital Marketing · 6 min read · By the Ila Fintech team

When someone nearby searches for "dental clinic in Lalitpur" or "laptop repair in Putalisadak", Google shows a map with three businesses at the top. Getting into that list is called local SEO, and most of it is free. These are the steps we recommend to every local business.

1. Claim your Google Business Profile

Search for your business on Google Maps. If it is there, claim it; if not, add it. Fill in every field: category, phone number, opening hours, website and real photos of your shop or office. Many streets in Kathmandu Valley have no official name, so place the map pin carefully and mention a nearby landmark or chowk in your address.

2. Ask happy customers for reviews

Reviews are one of the strongest signals Google uses for local results. Ask satisfied customers to leave one, and send them a direct link so it takes less than a minute. Reply politely to every review, including the bad ones.

3. Use the words your customers search for

Think about how people actually search: the service plus the area. Use those phrases naturally in your page titles, headings and text. Some customers search in Nepali or in romanised Nepali, so it can help to include common terms in both.

4. Keep your details the same everywhere

Your business name, address and phone number should match exactly on your website, Google profile, Facebook page and any directories. Small differences confuse Google and customers alike.

5. Make your website fast and mobile-friendly

Google favours sites that work well on phones. A slow, hard-to-read site will struggle to rank, however good your business is.

6. Keep posting

Add updates, offers and photos to your Google profile, and publish useful articles on your website. Fresh activity shows Google that the business is open and active.

How long does it take?

Local SEO usually takes a few weeks to a few months to show results, depending on your area and competition. Be careful with anyone who promises the number one spot: nobody can guarantee it.
